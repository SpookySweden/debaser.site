# DEBASER_OS design guide

Read this before any UI work. It is not needed for backend or logic tasks.

## Palette: "Dyacid Dyes"
- Nigrosine #1A1525
- Royal Blue #1D3CA6
- Daffodil Yellow #FFF000
- Flavine #E1FF00
- Brilliant Pink #FF00A0
- Rose #E6004C
- Emerald #28C745
- Plus black and white

Use with creative liberty. Do not force a rigid colour-to-role mapping.

## Feel
- Pacing: 90s hip-hop call-and-response. Transitions in the spirit of Samurai Champloo.
- NO smooth transitions or easing. Feedback is instant and mechanical: snap and inset clicks, like copper pressure contacts.
- Pixel-art accents.
- Moving away from the old Windows-95 look. Bevelled buttons are still in the code; no need to add more, and fine to replace them when touching something. A guide, not a hard rule.

## Checking visual work
- Take screenshots at desktop and phone width and compare them against what I asked for. List the differences and fix them.
- Run the screenshot QA audit for accessibility problems.
