## Crowd control
- Q (knock-up): Darkin Blade swings three times in areas, so step out of the arc of the swing you see coming, and keep your distance when Aatrox winds up.
- W (slow): Infernal Chains lands on the first enemy hit, so move out of the slam zone quickly, since champions get dragged back to the center for more damage.
- R (fear): World Ender fears nearby minions, not champions, so it does not stop you; watch the minion wave Aatrox is standing in when he transforms.
