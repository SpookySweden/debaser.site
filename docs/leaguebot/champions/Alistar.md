## Crowd control
- P (stun and knockback charge): Charges his roar when he stuns or displaces you or when nearby enemies die; stay out of his range during fights.
- Q (knock-up): Hits all nearby enemies at once, so step out of his close radius before he commits to the smash.
- W (knockback): Rams one target in a direct line; keep distance and do not be the lone target in front of him.
- E (stun): Trample ignores unit collision and stacks on champion hits; once full, his next basic attack stuns, so keep out of his attacks.
