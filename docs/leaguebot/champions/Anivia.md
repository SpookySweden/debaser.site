## Crowd control
- Q (stun and slow): The sphere flies in a line and stuns in a radius on explosion, so sidestep its path before it bursts.
- W (movement-blocking ice wall): The wall blocks all movement and melts after a short time, so step around it or wait for it to melt.
- R (slow storm): Glacial Storm damages and slows anyone in its area, so leave the storm zone to avoid the slow.
- CHECK BY HAND: Claude lists CC on W that Riot's text doesn't name plainly (often right: Riot's short text leaves effects out).
