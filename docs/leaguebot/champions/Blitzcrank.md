## Crowd control
- Q (pull): Rocket Grab is a straight hook that stops on the first champion it hits, so step out of its line or behind a minion.
- E (knock-up): Power Fist is applied on your next basic attack, so step back out of attack range when you see the charged fist.
- R (silence): Static Field is an active nearby burst that silences, so keep your distance until its area has passed.
