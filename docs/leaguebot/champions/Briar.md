## Crowd control
- Q (stun): Head Rush leaps to a unit, so step off the unit she is aiming at or keep a minion between you and her.
- W (taunt): Blood Frenzy hunts the nearest enemy and prioritizes champions, so keep a minion or tank closer to you than you are to her.
- E (stun, knockback, slow): A full charge knocks back and stuns against walls, so fight away from walls and step out when you see her charging the scream.
- R (fear): Certain Death marks the first champion her gemstone hits, so stay out of the kick line and do not stand near your prey.
