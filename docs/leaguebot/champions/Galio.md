## Crowd control
- W (taunt and slow): Galio slows while charging, then taunts nearby enemies on release, so step out of the radius before he lets go.
- E (knock-up): Galio's E knocks up the first champion he reaches after stepping back, so keep a minion or ally between you and him.
- R (knock-up): Galio's R lands on his chosen ally's location after a delay, so step away from the landing area before it smashes down.
