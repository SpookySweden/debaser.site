## Crowd control
- Q (slow on hit): Boomerang Throw flies in a line and returns; sidestep out of its path, and in Mega form the boulder stops on the first unit hit, so stand behind a minion.
- W (stun in mega form): Only Mega Gnar stuns, in the area in front of him; step back out of that area when he rears up to smash.
- E (slow on landing): Hop lands on a chosen spot and bounces off units he lands on, traveling further; do not stand under the landing spot.
- R (stun, slow and knockback): Mega form throws everything in a chosen direction; step off that line and avoid being pushed into a wall, which stuns you.
