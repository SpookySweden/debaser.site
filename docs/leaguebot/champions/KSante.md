## Crowd control
- Q (pull, slow, stun): Ntofo Strikes slows in a short line, and once fully stacked the shockwave pulls you in, so step off the line before he fires.
- W (stun, knockback): Path Maker charges first and then dashes in a line, so sidestep off that line once his charge begins instead of standing in front of him.
- R (knockback): All Out knocks an enemy back through walls in its path, so keep walls out of your back and avoid his line when he targets you.
