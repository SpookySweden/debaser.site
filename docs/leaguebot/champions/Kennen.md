## Crowd control
- P (stun from mark of the storm): Stuns after the third ability hit, so count marks on yourself and avoid taking a third spell hit in a fight.
- Q (marking shuriken): Thundering Shuriken flies toward a spot, so sidestep its line early; each hit adds a mark toward the stun.
- W (mark-applying surge): Electrical Surge adds marks to targets already marked, so break line of sight quickly when you see him activate it.
- E (marking dash): Lightning Rush passes through units and applies a mark, so stay near your frontline to limit where he can reach you.
- CHECK BY HAND: Claude lists CC on Q, W, E that Riot's text doesn't name plainly (often right: Riot's short text leaves effects out).
