## Crowd control
- P (slow): Frozen Thrall forms when an enemy champion dies near you, so kill enemies away from your zone to keep the thrall from slowing your team.
- Q (slow): Ice Shard is a straight spear that shatters on the first enemy, so sidestep sideways or dash out of its line to avoid it.
- W (root): Ring of Frost has no travel time and is centered on Lissandra, so back off early before she casts it.
- R (stun and slow): Frozen Tomb stuns an enemy champion, and on herself she is untargetable, so use it as a dodge rather than a block.
