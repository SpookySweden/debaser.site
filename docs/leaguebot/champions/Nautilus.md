## Crowd control
- P (root on first attack): Staggering Blow roots on his first auto, so stay out of his auto range before he attacks you and avoid giving him a first hit.
- Q (pull and stun): Dredge Line is a straight-line anchor skillshot, so sidestep off its line; hitting terrain pulls him toward it instead.
- E (slow): Riptide hits everything around him, so avoid standing inside his melee radius while his three waves are going off.
- R (stun and knock-up): Depth Charge chases one target through the earth, so respect it when he starts casting and fight away from him.
