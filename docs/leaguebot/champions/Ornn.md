## Crowd control
- Q (slow): The fissure runs in a line from Ornn toward the target area, so step sideways out of the line before the magma pillar forms at the end.
- W (root, knockback): Ornn advances in a straight line, so sidestep early, since the root and knockback only land on enemies caught in the final flame gout.
- E (knock-up): Searing Charge is a straight dash, and when Ornn hits a wall the shockwave knocks up nearby enemies, so avoid standing close to walls he can charge into.
- R (knock-up, slow): The elemental is visible and travels toward Ornn, so leave its path, and watch for his recast that redirects it to knock enemies up.
