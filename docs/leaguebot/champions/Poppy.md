## Crowd control
- Q (slow): Hammer Shock is a swing that creates a zone with a delayed explosion, so step out of the zone before the blast lands.
- W (grounded, slow): Steadfast Presence stops enemy dashes around Poppy, so avoid dashing into her area while she has it active.
- E (stun): Heroic Charge only stuns if you are pushed into a wall, so keep open ground behind you when Poppy dashes in.
