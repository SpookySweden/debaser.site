## Crowd control
- Q (disarm): Blinding Assault marks a chosen enemy and damages the area, so step out of the immediate area around her target.
- E (slow and knockback): Vault dashes to a chosen enemy and slows it, so avoid being on the line of her dash or pull back before she lands.
- R (blind): Behind Enemy Lines flies her around, then Skystrike blinds nearby enemies when she ends it, so look away from the Skystrike area.
