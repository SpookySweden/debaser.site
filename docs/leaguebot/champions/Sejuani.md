## Crowd control
- Q (knock-up charge): Charge is a straight line that stops on the first champion hit, so step sideways off its path before it connects.
- W (slow and knockback): Two swings hit the area in front of her, so stay out of melee range or behind minions when she winds up.
- E (stun on max frost stacks): Only hits enemies at max Frost stacks, so avoid taking repeated W hits to keep your Frost stacks low.
- R (stun bola and slowing ice storm): Bola is aimed at one target and freezes the first champion it hits, so sidestep the line or block it with an ally.
