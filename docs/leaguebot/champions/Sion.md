## Crowd control
- Q (stun, knock-up, slow): Decimating Smash is a visible windup in front of him; step sideways or back before release, since only a full charge stuns.
- E (knockback, slow): Roar of the Slayer is a short shockwave hitting the first enemy; sidestep the line or stand behind a minion, which is hit first.
- R (knock-up, stun, slow): Unstoppable Onslaught charges in a direction he steers toward your cursor; step out of its line, and stay close because knock-up scales with distance.
