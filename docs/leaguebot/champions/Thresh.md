## Crowd control
- Q (pull and stun): It targets one champion, so be out of his reach or make him spend it first; watch for his second cast that pulls him to you.
- E (slow and knockback): The sweep hits all enemies in its direction, so stay outside the arc of his chain and do not walk into the knockback line.
- R (slow walls): Walls form around the area; break them early to damage him and push out fast instead of waiting inside the prison he creates.
