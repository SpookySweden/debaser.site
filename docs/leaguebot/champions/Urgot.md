## Crowd control
- Q (slow): Aim the charge at the ground where enemies will stand, since the explosion lands on the point; lead moving targets.
- E (stun): Disdain is a straight charge, so sidestep out of its line before it arrives or keep distance until it dashes.
- R (suppression, fear, slow): Fear Beyond Death hits only the first champion in its path, so keep a minion or ally between you and the chem-drill.
